Typefaces

Two families, self hosted, subset to Latin. Archivo for everything readable, IBM Plex Mono for labels, numbers and code.

Archivo 400 to 800
Headings, body copy, buttons.
IBM Plex Mono 400 500
Eyebrows, figure captions, tabular numbers, code.

Usage rules

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the corner radius.
  • Do not recolour the mark, add a gradient, or add a glow.
  • Do not stretch it. Scale from a corner, uniformly.
  • Write the name as two words, capital P and F, no space inside.
