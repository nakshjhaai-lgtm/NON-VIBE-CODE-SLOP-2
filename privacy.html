Privacy policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

The short version

This site loads no analytics, sets no tracking cookies and requests nothing from a third party. Everything it remembers stays in your browser, and you can clear it with two clicks.

What is stored on your device

Three keys in browser storage, all of them written by this site and read only by this site:

Local and session storage keys
Key Contains Lifetime
pf.theme Light or dark theme choice Until you clear it
pf.cookies Whether you accepted or declined the banner Until you clear it
pf.campaign Campaign parameters from the link you arrived on This browser tab only

Cookies

None. The banner exists because a site that stores anything locally should say so, not because a tracker needs your permission. Declining changes nothing except hiding the banner.

Server logs

Whoever hosts these files may keep standard request logs, which usually include an IP address, the time and the path requested. That is a property of static hosting rather than something this site adds.

Forms

On this build, form submissions are validated in the browser and not transmitted. A production deployment would post to a form handler; when that changes, this page changes first.

Your rights

  • Clear everything this site stored by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Ask what we hold about you by writing to the address below.
  • Ask for anything we hold to be deleted.

Children

The editor is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the changelog gains an entry. Substantive changes are announced in the release note for that version.
