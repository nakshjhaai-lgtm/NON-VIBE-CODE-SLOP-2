Changelog

What changed, and when

Every release, newest first. Breaking changes are marked, and each one says what to do about it.

2.4.0 24 August 2026 Current

Instanced export and a smaller bundle

  • AddedInstanced meshes export to a single draw call in all six targets.
  • AddedThree levels of detail per mesh, swapped by screen height.
  • ChangedThe exporter now trims unused uniforms, cutting generated shaders by about a third.
  • FixedScene files saved while offline no longer lose their material assignments.
2.3.0 12 July 2026

Scroll timeline

  • AddedKeyframes measured in scroll percentage instead of seconds.
  • AddedNine easing curves, including a spring that needs no solver at runtime.
  • ChangedTimeline scrubbing no longer pauses the viewport.
  • FixedKeyframes at exactly 100 per cent now export.
2.2.1 3 June 2026

Safari shader fix

  • FixedFresnel nodes failed to compile on Safari 16.4 because of a reserved uniform name.
  • FixedExporting a scene with no camera produced an empty file instead of an error message.
  • ChangedError messages now name the node that caused them.
2.2.0 19 May 2026

Node materials

  • AddedTwenty four material nodes, including noise, gradient and custom GLSL.
  • AddedEvery node maps to one uniform in the exported shader.
  • BreakingMaterial files from 2.1 open read only. Re-save them to edit.
